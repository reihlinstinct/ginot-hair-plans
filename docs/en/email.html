Get a plan email

Draft machine translation, not yet reviewed by a native speaker.

Choose a plan in deposit and get an email with the plan details, the last day to file an objection and a link to the plan page on Mavat. To avoid sending to someone else's address, we first send a confirmation email, and the plan email is sent only after you press a button in it. The address is kept for up to 48 hours while waiting for confirmation and then deleted; if you ticked the subscription box it is kept (with the plan and language only) until you unsubscribe with the link in every email.
